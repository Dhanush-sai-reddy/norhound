# Signalpost recall v2 — design spec

Date: 19 Nov 2026. Owner: NorHound entry. Status: approved for implementation
("keep going", 19 Nov 2026).

## Why this revision exists

The shipped revision (`d16266a`) publishes **105,266 claims / 35,421 evidence
records** over 1,000 companies at precision **1.0** (0 wrong-company
publications). Two measured numbers describe it:

| measure | value | what it means |
|---|---|---|
| `scripts/measure_recall.py` six-field proxy | **22.3833 / 50** | our own strict proxy: six fields, each divided by 1,000 companies |
| `scripts/measure_family_recall.py` family analogue | **49.60 / 50** | per official fact family, company recall against the union's opportunity sets (the company-recall half of the rubric only) |

The rubric scores Recall/50 as **70 % company recall + 30 % individual-claim
recall** per external field family, against the union of every participant's and
Builderr's discoveries. The 22.38 figure is not that metric; it is our
conservative internal proxy. Its ceiling is arithmetic: `two_platforms`
(0.620) and `workforce_jobs` (0.014) are the only two fields with headroom, and
both are source-capped (every other keyless ratings/directory source was
measured and rejected — see `RECALL_LEVERS.md`).

Three measurements taken on 19 Nov (all reproducible, files under `out/`):

1. **Role cards exist.** 73 captured careers pages over 46 companies; **14
   companies** have a page that carries a role title *and* an apply action
   (`out/rolecard-hop-probe.json`). The shipped jobs connector
   (`extract_company_site_jobs.py`) published **zero** role cards — all 159 of
   its rows are `careers_page` — because its role-title vocabulary is too
   narrow ("Senior digital markedsfører", "Strukturert skrivetalent med teft
   for teknologi" are rejected) and it never fetches the role page.
2. **News depth is shallow.** 3,175 dated news claims over 1,000 companies
   (3.17 per company). The site's own sitemap lists **586 news-path URLs over
   89 companies**; **48 companies** have news-path URLs in their sitemap but
   publish no news fact at all, and 86 companies publish 194 items where the
   per-company caps (12 pages / 8 dated items) bind.
3. **The 374 directory-only companies are reachable through the register.**
   371 of them state a website domain on their directory page; **175** of those
   domains are declared by the same organisation number in the register's
   sole-declarer index (82,398 domains, 0 mismatches). A live probe of those
   175 fetched 72; **37 name the legal company and are not parked**.

## Goals

1. **Hiring company recall** — the only scored family below 99 % (49 / 84).
   Target: publish a real role card for every company whose verified site
   carries one (role title + apply action), never a bare careers page.
2. **Claim depth where the union is deep** — news (3.17 → target 5+ per
   covered company), website descriptions for the directory-only companies that
   can be proven, GLEIF identity for the 13 companies that hold an LEI.
3. **A second external platform for the directory-only companies** that can be
   proven, without widening the identity gate for anything else.
4. **Precision stays at 1.0** (≥ 0.95 gate, 0 wrong-company publications) and
   every published fact keeps source URL, retrieval time, reporting/effective
   date, content hash and extraction method.

## Non-goals

- No paid API, no login, no account, no keyless-source bypass of a site's
  terms. Cost per official batch stays **$0**.
- No widening of the website identity gate. New proofs are **new proof
  types**, each re-derived by the verifier from frozen evidence.
- No fabrication: a careers page is not a role card; headcount is not hiring;
  a parked domain is not a website.

## Levers

### A. Role cards from verified company sites (new connector)

New file `scripts/extract_company_site_role_cards.py`.

*Scope*: profiles whose website evidence is `available` **and**
`identity_assessment.publishable == true`.

*Discovery*: careers pages = captured pages whose path matches `CAREERS_PATH`
plus 8 canonical probes (`/karriere`, `/ledige-stillinger`, `/stillinger`,
`/jobber`, `/careers`, `/jobs`, `/join-us`, `/bli-en-av-oss`). From each
careers page, follow at most 12 **same registered-domain** links whose path
carries a vacancy token (`stilling|jobb|job|rekry|recruit|ledig|vakt|intern`)
or whose anchor text matches a role cue. Fetch every candidate role page.

*Role card acceptance* — all four must hold:

1. the page is HTML, on the verified site's registered domain (or subdomain);
2. a **role title** is present: JSON-LD `JobPosting.title`, else the page's
   `h1`/`h2`, else a featured-card line, else the URL slug; 3–120 characters,
   ≤ 14 words, ≥ 3 letters, not a generic label (`ledige stillinger`,
   `karriere`, `jobb hos oss`, `stillinger`, `vi søker`, `open positions`,
   `available positions`, `careers`, …) and not a negation
   (`ingen ledige stillinger`, `vi har ingen ledige stillinger`);
3. an **apply action** is present: an anchor/button whose label or href
   matches `søknad|soknad|apply|søk på stilling|stillingssøknad|send søknad|
   melde deg|søk stilling|send inn søknad`; a `mailto:` link with a subject
   counts; a same-page fragment (`#…soknad`) counts **only** when the page
   contains a `<form>` with a submit control;
4. **identity**: the page text states the organisation number, **or** ≥ half
   of the distinctive legal-name tokens (≥ 4 chars, legal forms dropped),
   **or** the host equals the domain BRREG declares for this organisation
   number.

*Emission*: `platform=company_site`, `signal_type=job_posting`,
`source_url` = the role page, `content_sha256` = SHA-256 of the fetched body,
`identity_proof` = `website_identity_gate` + `company_site_role_card`
(role title, apply URL). `observed_at` = a machine-readable publication
timestamp when the page states one, else a mined date marked
`date_precision=mined_from_text`. Max 8 role cards per company, sorted by URL
for byte-identical reruns.

### B. News depth from the site's own sitemap (modify the news connector)

`scripts/extract_company_site_news.py` gains `--sitemap <sitemap rows>`:
sitemap rows with `signal_type=sitemap_page` contribute their
`metrics.page_url` when the path matches `NEWS_PATH` and the host belongs to
the verified site. Candidate order: captured news pages (deepest first), then
sitemap URLs newest-first. `MAX_NEWS_PAGES` 12 → 24, `MAX_DATED_ITEMS` 8 → 16.
The dated rules are unchanged: the primary page may carry a mined date, every
additional page needs a machine-readable timestamp, undated navigation pages
never publish beyond one per company.

### C. A second platform for directory-only companies (new connector + verifier proof)

New file `scripts/extract_register_sole_declarer_sites.py`.

*Scope*: companies with **no** publishable captured website whose BRREG
declared host is in `data/registry-domain-unique.jsonl.gz` for that same
organisation number (the sole-declarer index: 82,398 domains, each declared by
exactly one organisation number).

*Acceptance*: HTTP 200 HTML, no parked/for-sale marker, and the page text
contains the organisation number or ≥ half of the distinctive legal-name
tokens. Emits `platform=company_site`, `signal_type=website_description`,
`content_sha256` of the fetched body, a bounded body excerpt, and
`identity_proof=[{"type": "registry_sole_declarer_domain_match", …}]`.

*Verifier* (`scripts/verify_and_label_observations.py`): a new proof kind
`registry_sole_declarer_domain_match`, trusted only when the proof names this
organisation, the source host equals the BRREG-declared host for it, the
sole-declarer index says the host belongs to this organisation alone, and the
observation's own stored body excerpt names the organisation. The verifier
takes `--domain-index`; without the index the proof is **not** trusted
(fail-safe). The existing `registry_declared_website_domain_match` path is
untouched.

### D. GLEIF identity (enable)

`run_gleif_connector.py` exists and is exact-entity by construction
(`registration.validatedAs` = organisation number, validated by
Brønnøysundregistrene). Enable `--gleif` in the official run: 13 of the 1,000
companies hold an LEI, 5 of them among the directory-only 374.

### E. ATS false positives (bug fix)

`run_ats_jobs_connector.py` marks jobbnorge navigation links as
`role_card: true`. Those rows are unpublished today (the fail-safe gate
rejects third-party boards), but the flag is wrong: require a role title or
JSON-LD `JobPosting` before a row is called a role card.

## Measurement and shipping

1. Fresh run: every input re-downloaded, every index rebuilt
   (`run_fresh_pipeline.sh` + the external pipeline with `--gleif` and the new
   connectors), request counter active.
2. Merge only real connector outputs (`finalize_run.sh` tag list extended
   with `rolecards`, `gleif`, `sole-declarer-sites`).
3. Re-verify, evaluate, emit envelopes, refresh against the 16 Nov baseline
   (idempotency check), rebuild summaries and the viewer.
4. Re-measure `measure_recall.py` and `measure_family_recall.py`; the numbers
   in `RECALL_*.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `README.md` and `SUBMISSION_EMAIL.md` are
   replaced with the measured values only.
5. Promote the package, commit, push `HEAD:main`, pin the corpus commit in
   `SUBMISSION_EMAIL.md` (docs-only follow-up commit) and send the revision
   email to `submit@builderr.ai`. The 19 Nov email is not re-sent; this is a
   new revision.

## Acceptance criteria

| criterion | target |
|---|---|
| entity precision | ≥ 0.99, **0** wrong-company publications |
| hiring family coverage | > 49 companies (target 55–62) |
| six-field proxy | ≥ 22.3833 (no regression) |
| family analogue | ≥ 49.60 / 50 |
| claim count | > 105,266 |
| runtime / cost | ≤ 12 min per 100 companies, $0 external |
| refresh idempotency | 0 new changes on a second pass, prior snapshots preserved |
| tests | full suite green (`uv run python -m unittest discover -s tests`) |

## Risks

| risk | mitigation |
|---|---|
| a group site publishes another entity's role or description | identity rule 4 (org number / legal-name tokens / BRREG-declared host) + manual sample of 10 published rows before promotion |
| the sole-declarer index is stale | the index is rebuilt in the fresh run; a domain declared by two organisations is excluded by construction |
| role-card titles are too loose (marketing copy) | generic-label and negation lists, ≤ 14 words, apply action required |
| news depth adds noise to the summaries | caps stay bounded (24 pages / 16 dated items); the summary builder reads counts, not raw items |
| runtime growth | connectors are bounded and parallel; the news/sitemap fetch budget is measured in the run report |
