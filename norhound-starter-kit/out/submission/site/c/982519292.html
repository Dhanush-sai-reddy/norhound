Oslo, Norway, 29 September 2026 – Element Logic has entered into a strategic global partnership with Ranpak, a leading provider of sustainable packaging automation solutions. The collaboration will integrate Ranpak’s end-of-line packaging automation technology into Element Logic’s warehouse automation portfolio, enabling customers to benefit from more efficient, scalable, and sustainable fulfilment solutions. As customer expectations continue to rise and new sustainability regulations such as the EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR) are introduced, businesses are looking for smarter ways to optimize warehouse operations from picking and packing through to shipping. Through this partnership, Element Logic customers will gain access to packaging automation technologies that can help reduce packaging material usage, lower shipped volume, and improve overall packing efficiency. Shared ambition The partnership builds on an existing relationship between the two companies and reflects a shared ambition to create more connected warehouse operations. By combining Element Logic’s expertise in warehouse automation, software, and systems integration with Ranpak’s capabilities in rightsizing, box forming, closing, computer vision, and paper-based product protection, customers can benefit from a more streamlined outbound logistics flow. Rather than treating packaging as a separate process, the integrated approach enables businesses to design picking, packing, and shipping as part of one cohesive operation. Ranpak’s automation technology replaces manual box-building with systems that automatically select, erect, and right-size packaging for each order, helping increase throughput, consistency, and transportation efficiency while reducing packaging waste. At Element Logic, we continuously expand our solution portfolio to help customers optimize warehouse performance from inbound processes to final shipment. With Ranpak playing a role in these integrations, Element Logic is offering their customers the pinnacle of flexibility and efficiency as they design and integrate intralogistics flows,” says Omar Asali, CEO of Ranpak. The partnership supports Element Logic’s ambition to deliver end-to-end warehouse solutions across the entire value chain, from inbound logistics and storage to order fulfilment, packaging, and final shipment. By broadening its solution portfolio, Element Logic continues to help customers improve operational performance while meeting evolving sustainability and business requirements.
