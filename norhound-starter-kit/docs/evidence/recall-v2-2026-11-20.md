## What changed

1. **Role cards from verified company sites** — 20 role cards over 8 companies,
   each with a role title and an apply action. A role-card title must name a
   role (role-token rule); a careers page alone is never a job posting; a page
   naming another legal entity is rejected before the host-only proof.
2. **Sitemap news depth** — per-company sitemap cap 10 → 60, news candidates
   12 → 24, dated items 8 → 16. News observations 194 over 86 companies → **490
   over 140 companies, 447 dated**. Sitemap observations 2,475 → **10,389**.
3. **Register sole-declarer sites** — a second external platform for 10
   directory-only companies whose register-declared domain names them, proved
   by the register's sole-declarer index and the page body.
4. **GLEIF identity** — 13 companies with a Brønnøysund-validated LEI now
   publish; the verifier re-derives the proof's org number, authority and body
   hash (the v1 connector emitted these rows but the verifier rejected them).
5. **ATS false positives** — jobbnorge navigation links are no longer flagged
   as role cards.
